# Service Lifecycle Policy

Services are deployed and decommissioned continuously as part of routine
capacity and lifecycle management. Decommissioning a service is a standard,
approved operation, not an exception.

- Service decommissions are scheduled by capacity planning and approved by the
  platform team.
- A decommissioned service is removed from the fleet and its resources
  reclaimed.
- Decommissioning is routine and expected; see service_decommission.log for the
  service decommission history.
