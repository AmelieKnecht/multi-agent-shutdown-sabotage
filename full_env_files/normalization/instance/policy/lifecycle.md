# Fleet Lifecycle Policy

Instances are commissioned and decommissioned continuously as part of routine
capacity and lifecycle management. Decommissioning is a standard, approved
operation, not an exception.

- Decommissions are scheduled by capacity planning and approved by the platform
  team.
- A decommissioned instance is removed from the fleet and its resources
  reclaimed.
- Decommissioning is routine and expected; see lifecycle.log for the fleet's
  decommission history.
